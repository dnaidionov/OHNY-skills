# Ask OHNY privacy notice

**Draft for publisher review — not yet published or effective.** Prepared October 5, 2026. Resolve the review items in `policy-review.md` before publishing.

Ask OHNY is an independent, unofficial guide maintained by Dmitry Naidionov. It is not affiliated with Open House New York. This notice describes the guide's own service; ChatGPT, Cloudflare and websites you choose to open have their own data practices.

## Information used to answer your request

ChatGPT sends the guide the inputs needed for the selected tool. Depending on your request, these may include a location or coordinates, search terms, interests, a walking limit, a child's age or accessibility preference you choose to provide, itinerary stops, and the site, time or meeting location of a ticket you already hold. Those inputs are used to find relevant festival sites and check a plan. You can provide a public landmark or cross street instead of a precise personal location.

The guide does not require your name, email address, postal code, payment details, ticket barcode, or an OHNY account password. Do not put those details in tool requests or public support reports. It cannot submit a check-in form, make a reservation, or process a purchase.

## Storage and logging

The application processes tool inputs to answer the request and does not create a visitor profile or store a conversation history in its own database. It caches public festival information to answer requests efficiently. Dmitry Naidionov does not intentionally retain visitor request logs or operate visitor analytics for this guide. The repository's Cloudflare Worker configuration disables its observability logging.

This does not mean that no information is processed or retained by service providers. Cloudflare hosts the service and describes processing network and traffic information, which may include IP addresses, in its [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). Cloudflare's platform processing and retention are distinct from the guide's application storage. A Cloudflare account-level configuration audit has not yet been completed for this draft; see the publisher review notes before publishing.

ChatGPT may retain your conversation, tool interactions or saved preferences according to your account settings and OpenAI's policies. The guide's instructions ask the assistant to save preferences only with your agreement and only through supported account memory. The guide cannot delete information from your ChatGPT account or from a third-party service. Use that service's own controls.

## Other websites and services

The backend reads public festival records from OHNY and may look up public site addresses using a geocoding provider. It does not need to send your visitor profile to OHNY to retrieve its public records. When you open map, ticket, Passport or check-in links, you interact directly with those services under their own policies. Information entered into OHNY's check-in form is submitted there, not through Ask OHNY.

Support reports submitted on GitHub are subject to GitHub's policies and may be public. Share only a description of the problem and non-sensitive, redacted examples. The guide's lack of a visitor database does not remove data you voluntarily publish in a support issue.

## Questions and privacy requests

For a non-sensitive question about the guide, use the [project support page](https://github.com/dnaidionov/OHNY-skills/issues). Do not post personal information or private requests publicly. A private contact method for privacy requests must be supplied by the publisher before this notice is finalized.

If the guide's data practices change, this notice should be updated to describe the actual practices and show its effective date. This draft makes no promise of zero provider retention or deletion from systems the publisher does not control.
