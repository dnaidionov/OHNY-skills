Ask OHNY is an independent guide maintained by Dmitry Naidionov for Open House New York Weekend, October 16–18, 2026. Open House New York did not create, endorse or sponsor this guide.

## What the guide provides

The guide helps find participating sites, read public festival information, plan visits around tickets you already hold, and open directions and check-in links. It provides information and suggestions; it does not issue tickets, reserve admission, submit check-ins, or act as an event organizer.

Hours, entry rules, cancellations, accessibility information and ticket availability can change. Check the official event or venue page before relying on an answer. Walking and travel times are estimates, and directions links should be checked for current conditions. A suggestion does not guarantee admission, an available ticket, a particular queue length or an accessible route.

## Tickets, forms and third-party services

Any purchase, reservation or check-in you complete through an external link is handled by that external service under its terms. Ask OHNY cannot complete or refund such transactions. Questions about admission or event policies should go to OHNY or the venue.

Use of ChatGPT, map services, Cloudflare-hosted pages and other linked services is also subject to their applicable terms. This guide does not promise that every ChatGPT plan, device, mode or country supports it. Supported mobile and voice behavior must be stated according to actual testing.

## Availability and appropriate use

This release is seasonal. It offers festival tools through October 18, 2026; site questions only from October 19 through November 18; and no tools from November 19. The service may be unavailable because of outages or changes in its upstream data or host platform.

Use it for festival-related assistance. Do not use the service to interfere with systems, bypass access controls or send information that you do not have authority to share. Do not submit credentials, payment information or ticket barcodes to the guide.
